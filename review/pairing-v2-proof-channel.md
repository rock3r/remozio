# Authenticated Transport-to-Authority pairing proof

Part of #852. Design for review against `main` at `ea1b3f4c`. This slice adds the pairing proof path. Installed v2 activation, LAN exposure, and the attended release gate need separate approval. [Activation checklist](https://github.com/rock3r/remozio/issues/852#issuecomment-7126562929)

## Peer checks and ownership

The signed app opens and owns the v2 window through its existing Authority connection. It gives Transport only the public window projection. The full invitation goes to the signed app for the user to share with the phone. Administrator consent, transcript comparison, the phone's biometric proof, durable commit, and receipt recovery keep their existing owners.

Use the existing Transport-facing Authority Mach service with a versioned proof extension and a dedicated, short-lived XPC connection. Before starting the listener, Transport authenticates Authority through the privileged Mach service, the configured Authority signing requirement, and the kernel-reported Root UID. A harmless version exchange precedes all proof material. Use the protected service name, expected identifier, signing team, and approved code hashes from the existing Transport startup configuration. An unavailable version fails setup and carries no proof. [Client](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityXPCChannel.swift)

Authority applies the configured Transport signing requirement before accepting or activating the connection. Require the dedicated Transport UID and any configured audit session. Check the actual current XPC connection synchronously on every exported invocation. Recheck the active Transport role, retained signing policy and revision before admitting, confirming, and consuming a proof. Keep the App and Setup service interfaces closed to this connection. Bind the dedicated connection to proof use at negotiation and refuse unrelated operations on it. The proof interface grants no enrollment, approval, or administrative right. [Peer checks](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/XPCPeerPolicy.swift) · [Transport role](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityTransportAccess.swift)

Construct one pairing owner in the Root runtime and inject it into the app and Transport-facing listeners before either starts. Keep the windows and version exclusions in `AuthorityPairingSetupAccess`. Bind a proof connection to one live app-owned window and the current Authority and Transport connection incarnations. Keep this state in memory. Reject requests for missing windows, other connections, or retired policy. [Root composition](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityWakeService.swift) · [Current window](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityPairingV2Window.swift)

## Proof exchange

1. At TCP accept, Transport allocates the existing local connection ID and records the original three-second deadline on a sleep-inclusive monotonic clock. The phone pins the Transport TLS identity. Transport requires TLS 1.3 and the v2 ALPN, then reads the candidate public key and the 32-byte pairing exporter from that connection's TLS metadata. Keep the existing source and slot limits.
2. On the authenticated proof link, register the live window handle, local connection ID, accept time, TLS peer key, and exporter. Authority records those fields under that link's incarnation and returns a fresh candidate handle and 32-byte challenge. Check the accept sample against the same host monotonic clock. Reject a future sample or expired deadline and cap the deadline by the window's expiry. The challenge starts no new timer.
3. Transport sends that challenge on the same TLS connection and forwards the bounded canonical phone proposal and its invitation proof tag under the same candidate handle. Authority uses its retained challenge, exporter, and invitation to verify the existing v2 HMAC. The tag binds the public invitation, exact proposal, challenge, and exporter. Require the proposal's transport key to match the registered TLS peer key. Recheck the signed app owner, current role, connection incarnation, scope, re-pair predecessor, and deadline. A failed proof retires its candidate. A valid proof claims the window once.
4. Keep the local slot in verification until Authority confirms it. Transport rechecks the original slot and deadline before confirming the reservation and again before publishing the candidate. Authority requires the same link and a live candidate at confirmation. The signed app can stage only a confirmed candidate from its own window with the exact canonical proposal. It consumes that reservation once. A late reply, retired slot, or lost confirmation closes the candidate. After a successful proof, any ambiguous failure requires a new window.

The proof channel carries these public claims, connection handles, deadlines, the challenge, exporter, and tag. It carries no invitation secret, full invitation, derived proof key, private key, or administrator authorization. Reuse the v2 wire format. Bound the XPC envelope before decode and enforce canonical fields and fixed widths. Drop retained proof inputs when consumed or retired. Keep them out of errors, logs, and retained records. [Proof input](../protocol/swift/Sources/RemozioProtocol/PairingInvitationV2.swift) · [Phone prelude](../protocol/swift/Sources/RemozioProtocol/PairingSetupPreludeV2.swift)

Authority verifies the phone's possession proof independently. The binding to the actual socket depends on the authenticated Transport process reading that socket's TLS metadata. Root has no independent view of the socket. Code signing and HMAC validation do not attest the socket against a compromised, policy-approved Transport process. If that stronger property is required for activation, it needs a different TLS ownership design.

## Retirement and capacity

An unsigned peer, wrong binary, wrong UID, or wrong XPC invocation cannot negotiate or allocate proof state. A Transport restart, XPC interruption, stale policy, or app close retires the affected link and its reservations. Bind cleanup to the original incarnations so a late callback cannot touch a new window. Stop the listener and drain its actual handlers. Require a new app-owned window, fresh XPC authentication, and a new TLS exchange after loss. Reject uncommitted staging or commit after retirement. Preserve any already committed result for the existing receipt recovery.

Keep at most four incomplete candidates per window, one successful reservation, and bounded draining work. Count proof work against a dedicated Authority budget before decoding or verification. Release capacity when work returns. Use the existing connection registry and admission controls. Account for opening, handshake, verification, and draining. Reserve the proof connection only where protected configuration leaves room without consuming approval, recovery, or replacement capacity. The current eight-connection default has no guaranteed spare slot at full use. Keep the extension unavailable when capacity cannot be guaranteed. A deployment capacity change needs review before activation. [Connection budget](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityXPCListener.swift)

## Gate proposal

- Prove the normal first enrollment and re-pair over the actual loopback TLS path. Check that staging rejects a different proposal, owner, or reservation. Exercise expiry at the original accept deadline through delayed registration, verification, and confirmation.
- Test each mismatched binding in isolated fixtures. Cover malformed frames, stale trust or code policy, duplicate calls, wrong signing policy or UID, closed app and Transport links, retired callbacks, and restart with a new incarnation. Verify bounded work and that approval and recovery keep their capacity. Check that the proof link has no app or Setup operations.
- Use the full pairing classes from #950. Run `AuthorityPairingSetupTests`, `AuthorityJournalSchema19MigrationTests`, `PairingSetupNetworkSessionTests`, `TransportPairingControlTests`, `MacPairingSessionTests`, `PairingSetupV2AdmissionsTests`, and `AndroidPairingMacLoopbackTests` with its local Swift peer. Add the full affected classes `AuthorityXPCChannelTests`, `AuthorityXPCEndpointTests`, `AuthorityXPCListenerTests`, `XPCPeerPolicyTests`, `AuthorityPresenceXPCTests`, `AuthorityPresenceChannelTests`, `AuthorityServiceTests`, `AuthorityWakeStartupSettingsTests`, and `AuthorityMaintenancePairingAdmissionTests`. Include any new proof channel class in full. Add the applicable TrustFeed or runtime classes if the implementation changes their wiring.
- Compile and discover under ATC. Send the exact diff, discovered selectors, estimate, and cap before executing tests. Gate estimates depend on the implementation and the discovered set. Test native process identity with the signed, installed Authority and Transport under a separate attended gate. Logical policy fixtures and local TLS cannot establish the installed signing, service identity, or physical device behavior.

The review needs agreement on the dedicated connection and protected capacity rule, the accept-time clock across both processes, and the confirmation before candidate publication. This branch contains the plan only.
