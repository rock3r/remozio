## Peer checks and ownership

The signed app opens and owns the v2 window through its existing Authority connection. It gives Transport only the public window projection. The full invitation goes to the signed app for the user to share with the phone. Administrator consent, transcript comparison, the phone's biometric proof, durable commit, and receipt recovery keep their existing owners.

Use the existing Transport-facing Authority Mach service for version negotiation and to obtain a dedicated proof-only XPC connection. Transport authenticates Authority through the privileged service, the configured signing requirement, and the kernel-reported Root UID. Use the protected service name, identifier, signing team, and approved code hashes from Transport startup configuration. Authority returns a short-lived anonymous XPC listener endpoint over that authenticated link. The dedicated connection verifies both peers again and uses a harmless hello before any proof data. The separate endpoint routes proof traffic outside the general connection registry, even when all eight general slots are occupied. [Client](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityXPCChannel.swift)

Authority applies the configured Transport signing requirement before accepting or activating the connection. Require the dedicated Transport UID and any configured audit session. Check the actual current XPC connection synchronously on every exported invocation. Recheck the active Transport role, retained signing policy and revision before admitting, confirming, and consuming a proof. Keep the App and Setup service interfaces closed to this connection. Bind the dedicated connection to proof use at negotiation and refuse unrelated operations on it. The proof interface grants no enrollment, approval, or administrative right. [Peer checks](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/XPCPeerPolicy.swift) · [Transport role](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityTransportAccess.swift)

Construct one pairing owner in the Root runtime and inject it into the app and Transport-facing listeners before either starts. Keep the windows and version exclusions in `AuthorityPairingSetupAccess`. Bind a proof connection to one live app-owned window and the current Authority and Transport connection incarnations. Keep this state in memory. Reject requests for missing windows, other connections, or retired policy. ## Proof exchange

1. At TCP accept, Transport allocates the existing local connection ID and records the original three-second deadline on a sleep-inclusive monotonic clock. The phone pins the Transport TLS identity. Transport requires TLS 1.3 and the v2 ALPN, then reads the candidate public key and the 32-byte pairing exporter from that connection's TLS metadata. Keep the existing source and slot limits.
2. On the authenticated proof link, register the live window handle, local connection ID, accept time, TLS peer key, and exporter. Authority records those fields under that link's incarnation and returns a fresh candidate handle and 32-byte challenge. Check the accept sample against the same host monotonic clock. Reject a future sample or expired deadline and cap the deadline by the window's expiry. The challenge starts no new timer. Check again before recording registration or returning its challenge. Reject an accept-time sample in the future, expiry during registration, and a deadline past the window's end.
3. Admit one registration per link incarnation and local connection ID. Refuse a repeated ID or an exporter already bound to another ID on that link, including retired candidates. Keep a bounded in-memory record of used bindings for the link's life and refuse further registrations when it fills. The registered TLS key and exporter stay fixed through confirmation. Transport sends the challenge on the same TLS connection and forwards the bounded canonical phone proposal and its invitation proof tag under the same candidate handle. Authority uses its retained challenge, exporter, and invitation to verify the existing v2 HMAC. The tag binds the public invitation, exact proposal, challenge, and exporter. Require the proposal's transport key to match the registered TLS peer key. Recheck the signed app owner, current role, connection incarnation, scope, re-pair predecessor, and deadline. A failed proof retires its candidate. A valid proof claims the window once.
4. Keep the local slot in verification until Authority confirms it. Transport rechecks the original slot and deadline before confirming the reservation and again before publishing the candidate. Authority requires the same link and a live candidate at confirmation. The signed app can stage only a confirmed candidate from its own window with the exact canonical proposal. It consumes that reservation once. A late reply, retired slot, or lost confirmation closes the candidate. After a successful proof, any ambiguous failure requires a new window.

The proof channel carries these public claims, connection handles, deadlines, the challenge, exporter, and tag. It carries no invitation secret, full invitation, derived proof key, private key, or administrator authorization. Reuse the v2 wire format. Bound the XPC envelope before decode and enforce canonical fields and fixed widths. Drop retained proof inputs when consumed or retired. Keep them out of errors, logs, and retained records. [Proof input](../protocol/swift/Sources/RemozioProtocol/PairingInvitationV2.swift) · [Phone prelude](../protocol/swift/Sources/RemozioProtocol/PairingSetupPreludeV2.swift)

Authority verifies the phone's possession proof independently. The binding to the actual socket depends on the authenticated Transport process reading that socket's TLS metadata. Root has no independent view of the socket. A compromised, policy-approved Transport already holds the TLS key the phone pins and can misreport the socket binding. Authority still requires the invitation proof. Record this accepted limit in the final PR's security documentation.

## Retirement and capacity

An unsigned peer, wrong binary, wrong UID, or wrong XPC invocation cannot negotiate or allocate proof state. A Transport restart, XPC interruption, stale policy, or app close retires the affected link and its reservations. Bind cleanup to the original incarnations so a late callback cannot touch a new window. Stop the listener and drain its actual handlers. Require a new app-owned window, fresh XPC authentication, and a new TLS exchange after loss. Reject uncommitted staging or commit after retirement. Preserve any already committed result for the existing receipt recovery.

Reserve one proof slot outside the eight general connections. Only an authenticated Transport role, UID, and signing identity can obtain that slot. Count the pending endpoint, its handshake, and the live link against the same slot. Refuse a second concurrent link, with no replacement or eviction. An unused endpoint expires at its handshake deadline. Interruption or invalidation retires that incarnation and releases the slot when its work settles. A late event from the old link cannot release a new slot. Approval, receipt recovery, and the replacement margin keep their current budgets.

Keep at most four incomplete candidates per window, one successful reservation, and bounded draining work. Count proof work against a dedicated Authority budget before decoding or verification. Release work capacity when the work returns. [Connection budget](../macos/core/Sources/RemozioCore/AuthorityXPCListener.swift)
